Terms, pricing and refunds

Template - have this reviewed by a lawyer before you launch, and fill in your legal entity name, address, contact and grievance details. Payment gateways (including Razorpay) check that these pages exist, so keep them accurate.

1. The service

DhanKhoj is an information service. It compares your name, PIN code and folio with unpaid / unclaimed dividend lists that companies publish, and tells you about possible matches and estimated IEPF transfer dates. It is not affiliated with SEBI, the IEPF Authority, any stock exchange or any company, and it is not legal, tax or investment advice. Matches are estimates and may be wrong or incomplete. We do not guarantee that any dividend can be claimed.

2. Accounts

You must be 18 or older and give your own correct details (name as on your shares, mobile number, email, and the PAN you add in your profile to start the search). One account per person: an email address, PAN or mobile number can be registered once. A saved PAN cannot be changed by you; write to us if it is wrong. You are responsible for keeping your password secret. We may suspend accounts that give false details or misuse the service.

3. Free plan and memberships

A free account shows full details for up to the number of companies stated on the pricing page (currently 5 companies), with email alerts. The Silver, Gold and Platinum memberships raise the company limit and add SMS / WhatsApp alerts as described on the pricing page, for the period you buy (one month or one year; the yearly price is discounted). The plan, price and period are shown before you pay. Prices include GST where applicable. Memberships do not renew automatically. If you switch plan while one is running, the days left are converted into days of the new plan in proportion to their monthly prices. When a membership ends your account returns to the free plan.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by our payment gateway partner (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets) or by direct UPI transfer to our UPI ID. We do not store your card or UPI PIN details. A direct UPI payment is activated after we match its UTR with our bank statement.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Set your own policy and state it clearly here. Suggested wording to adapt: if you were charged but the plan did not activate, or you were charged twice, we refund the amount to the original payment method within 7 working days of you writing to us. Because a membership gives immediate access to information, we do not refund a membership that has been activated and used, except where required by law. Refund requests: use the support email shown on the pricing page.

6. Expert help (optional)

If you ask for help from a verified professional on our panel, they quote their fee in writing first and nothing is charged until you accept. The professional is independent of DhanKhoj; the engagement is between you and them.

7. Liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses from relying on a match, an estimate or a deadline shown on this site. Always confirm with the company or its registrar.

8. Contact and grievances

Add your legal entity name, registered address, support email and phone, and the name and email of your grievance officer. Governing law: India; courts at your city.
