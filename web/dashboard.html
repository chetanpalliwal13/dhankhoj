Weaker possible matches

Amounts are hidden for weak matches because they may belong to someone else. These share parts of your name but PIN / folio did not agree. Add your folio or PIN under Profile to confirm or remove them.

What to do next

  1. Check the folio number against your own records or the company's list.
  2. Contact the company's registrar (RTA) with folio, PAN, ID proof and a cancelled cheque.
  3. If the date has passed, the money may be with the IEPF: file Form IEPF-5.
