Quick answers

I did not get my confirmation email

Check spam, then sign in and choose Resend. Make sure the address was typed correctly.

A match does not look like me

Matches are estimates. Check the folio and address, and compare with the company's official list before acting. Tell us and we will look into it.

How do I claim a dividend?

Read the How to claim guide. If the money has moved to the IEPF, the claim is made on the IEPF website.

My payment went through but my plan did not change

Write to us with the payment reference or UTR. UPI payments are approved after we check the bank statement.

Can I change my PAN?

No. Your PAN and IDs are locked once saved, to protect your account. Write to us if you made a mistake.
