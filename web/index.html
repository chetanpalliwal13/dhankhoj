Questions people ask

Is this list from SEBI?

No. SEBI does not publish one national list. Each listed company must publish its own unpaid-dividend statement on its website (and on a government-approved site), and after 7 years the money is transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). We gather company lists; coverage grows as lists are added, so a "no match" does not prove you have nothing unclaimed.

What happens after 7 years?

The dividend, and often the shares too if nothing was claimed for 7 consecutive years, move to the IEPF. The company list stops showing them, but you can still claim from the IEPF using Form IEPF-5. Step-by-step guide.

How accurate is the match?

Matching uses your PAN, name, PIN and any demat (DP-Client) IDs or folio numbers you add. Two people can share a name, so always verify the folio against your own records. We label results high / medium / low confidence and alert only on the first two.

Do you charge anything or help claim for a fee?

Creating an account and seeing your first 5 companies is free, with email alerts. If more companies match you, choose Silver, Gold or Platinum (monthly or yearly, with a discount for yearly) and pay by UPI, card or netbanking - no auto-renewal. You can always claim from the company or the IEPF yourself at no cost - our guide shows how. If you would rather not handle the paperwork, you can optionally ask a verified CS / CA from our panel to do it for a fee quoted in writing first. Be wary of anyone who promises to "release" your dividend and asks for OTPs or upfront money.

Is my data safe?

Public search shows masked names only. Full details appear only after you sign in with your password and confirm your email. Your PAN is stored encrypted and shown masked. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time.
