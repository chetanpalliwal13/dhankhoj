Privacy notice

Template - have this reviewed by a lawyer against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before you launch publicly.

What we collect

Required to register: first and last name, mobile number, email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash). Required to run the search: your PAN, which you add in your profile. Optional, if you add them: up to 5 demat (DP-Client) IDs or folio numbers, middle name, date of birth, father's / spouse's name, address, city, state, PIN code, alternate mobile, other spellings of your name, and preferred language. Records we keep about your use: when you accepted the terms, each time you switched an alert channel on or off (with the date and IP address, as proof of consent), sign-in sessions, and payment records. We do not ask for Aadhaar, bank account or card numbers.

Why

To match you against published unpaid-dividend lists, to confirm your email and mobile, to send the alerts and deadline reminders you chose by email, SMS and WhatsApp, to take payment for memberships, and to keep records that help you or a professional you engage prepare a claim. Your PAN is the unique key for the search (it is also compared with PANs printed in company lists) and keeps one account per person; it is also kept as a record for claims; it is stored encrypted and shown to you masked.

Public search and the free limit

The free check on the home page shows masked names, the last 4 characters of a folio and an amount range. After you sign in, full details are shown for the number of companies your plan includes; the rest are shown only as a count.

Cookies

We set one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing

If you request expert help, your name, contact details and the selected dividend entries (with full folio numbers) are shared with the professional assigned to you - only after you tick the consent box. Payments are handled by our payment gateway, which receives the details needed to process the payment. Messages are delivered by email, SMS and WhatsApp providers acting for us. We do not sell data. Experts who apply to our panel give us their name, membership number, contact details and city, which are listed publicly once verified.

Retention and your rights

Accounts whose email is never confirmed are deleted after 48 hours. You can correct your details, switch any alert channel off (also with the link in every message), or delete your account from the Account page; deletion removes your profile, PAN, matches, alerts, sign-ins and consent records immediately. Payment records (amount, date, receipt number) are kept without your personal details for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Operator contact / grievance officer: add your details here.
