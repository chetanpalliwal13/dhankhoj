We make forgotten money easy to find.

Every year, dividends that investors never collected are moved to a government fund after seven years. The lists exist, but they are scattered across thousands of company sites and PDFs. DhanKhoj reads them for you and tells you when your name appears.

What we do

We read the lists

We collect the unpaid-dividend lists companies publish and turn them into one searchable record, with the source and date kept for each list.

We match carefully

Your PAN, demat or folio ID, name and address are compared with each record. Every match shows how strong it is and why it matched, so you can judge it.

We tell you in time

You get an alert when something appears, and reminders as the estimated transfer date to the IEPF gets closer.

How we work

Privacy first

Your PAN is encrypted at rest. Public searches show only masked details. We never sell your data.

Honest about limits

Matches are estimates from published lists and can be incomplete. We tell you when a match is uncertain and point you to the official sources to confirm.

You keep the money

Claiming from a company or the IEPF is free. We explain the steps, and any expert help is optional and quoted before it starts.
