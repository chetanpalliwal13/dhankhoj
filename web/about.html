We make forgotten money easy to find.

Every year, dividends that investors never collected are moved to a government fund after seven years. The lists exist, but they are scattered across thousands of company sites and PDFs. DhanKhoj reads them for you and tells you when your name appears.

Why DhanKhoj exists

Money gets left behind

People change addresses, switch phone numbers, lose old share certificates or inherit shares they never knew about. A dividend sent to an old bank account bounces back, and the company keeps it in an unpaid dividend account.

Then the clock starts

If nobody claims it for seven years, the amount, and often the shares too, move to the government's Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). It can still be claimed, but the process is longer. The earlier you find it, the simpler it is.

The lists are public, but they sit on thousands of separate company sites and PDFs. Nobody should have to read all of them. That is the work DhanKhoj does for you.

Our mission

What we are building

One place where an Indian investor can check their name, see what may be waiting for them, and know exactly what to do next, without paying an agent or handing over sensitive documents.

What we believe

Your money is yours. Finding it should be free to start, easy to understand and honest about what we do not know.

What we do

We read the lists

We collect the unpaid-dividend lists companies publish and turn them into one searchable record, with the source and date kept for each list.

We match carefully

Your PAN, demat or folio ID, name and address are compared with each record. Every match shows how strong it is and why it matched, so you can judge it.

We tell you in time

You get an alert when something appears, and reminders as the estimated transfer date to the IEPF gets closer.

How we work

Privacy first

Your PAN is encrypted at rest. Public searches show only masked details. We never sell your data.

Honest about limits

Matches are estimates from published lists and can be incomplete. We tell you when a match is uncertain and point you to the official sources to confirm.

You keep the money

Claiming from a company or the IEPF is free. We explain the steps, and any expert help is optional and quoted before it starts.

What DhanKhoj is not

  • We are an independent information service. We are not part of SEBI, the IEPF Authority, any stock exchange or any company.
  • We do not release dividends or promise to. The money is claimed from the company or the IEPF, and our guide shows how.
  • We never ask for OTPs, card numbers or upfront money to "release" a dividend. If someone does in our name, do not pay and tell us.
